# Code of Conduct

Markspread adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
without modification. The full canonical text is at the link above
and reproduced in summary form below.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We pledge to act in
ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by mistakes,
  and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for
  the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour are listed in section 1 of the
[canonical text](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
We adopt that list verbatim.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our
standards of acceptable behaviour and will take appropriate and fair
corrective action in response to any behaviour that they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit,
or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other
contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and
will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces — the
GitHub organisation, the issue tracker, pull requests, the Discord
server, the Mastodon and X accounts, and any in-person event held
under the Markspread name — and also applies when an individual is
officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report concerns to <conduct@markspread.app>. The address goes to a
small group of maintainers; reports are not visible to the wider
contributor base.

What to expect:

- Acknowledgement within **2 business days**.
- A first-pass response (clarifying questions or initial action)
  within **7 days**.
- A resolution or status update within **30 days**. If we cannot
  reach a resolution in that window, we tell you why before it
  expires.

We will respect the reporter's privacy and security throughout.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders will follow the four-step ladder defined in the
[canonical Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):

1. **Correction** — private written warning, with clarity about the
   violation and an explanation of why the behaviour was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour;
   no interaction with the people involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from any sort of interaction
   or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban from any sort of public
   interaction within the community.

Which step applies depends on the severity and pattern of the
violation. The maintainers' decision is final but documented; the
reporter and the subject of the report both receive a written
explanation.
